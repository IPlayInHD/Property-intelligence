# PropIQ User Research Plan

**Objective:** Validate whether target users would pay for and regularly use PropIQ — a UAE property intelligence platform — before significant engineering investment.

**Research Question:** Do UAE property investors, agents, and first-time buyers experience pain severe enough with existing tools (Bayut, Propertyfinder) that they would adopt and pay for PropIQ's data-driven analysis layer?

---

## Research Goals

1. **Validate the problem** — Confirm users genuinely struggle with pricing transparency, rental yield accuracy, and neighbourhood quality assessment.
2. **Validate the solution** — Determine if PropIQ's 6 analytical modules address those pains directly.
3. **Validate willingness to pay** — Test the AED 30/month (Analyst) and AED 150/month (Pro) price points.
4. **Identify the beachhead segment** — Find which user type (investor, agent, first-time buyer) has the highest urgency.
5. **Surface objections** — Uncover trust, data accuracy, and switching-cost concerns early.

---

## Target Segments

| Segment | Description | Research Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Active Investors** | Individuals managing 2+ UAE properties or actively searching to buy | In-depth interview (30 min) |
| **Real Estate Agents** | Licensed UAE agents handling 5+ transactions per year | In-depth interview (30 min) |
| **First-Time Buyers** | People in active search for their first UAE property | Short interview (20 min) |
| **General UAE Residents** | Renters or property-curious population | Online survey |

---

## Recruitment Criteria

- **Active Investors:** Must have bought/sold UAE property in the last 3 years OR currently searching. Age 28–60.
- **Real Estate Agents:** Licensed RERA agent, active on Bayut or Propertyfinder listings.
- **First-Time Buyers:** Actively searching in the last 6 months, household income AED 15k+/month (can afford property in Dubai/Abu Dhabi).
- **General Survey:** UAE resident 22+, has rented or considered buying UAE property.

**Target Sample:**
- 5–8 in-depth interviews per segment (15–24 interviews total)
- 150–300 survey responses

---

## Timeline

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Recruit participants, finalise interview guide and survey |
| Week 2 | Run investor and agent interviews |
| Week 3 | Run first-time buyer interviews, launch online survey |
| Week 4 | Analyse all data, synthesise findings, present recommendations |

---

## Success Criteria (Go / No-Go)

- **Go signal:** 60%+ of interviewed investors/agents rate the pain as "significant" and 40%+ say they would trial PropIQ at the Analyst price.
- **Go signal:** Survey NPS proxy (would recommend concept) ≥ 30.
- **No-go signal:** Users don't trust third-party valuation data OR already have an internal process they're satisfied with.

---

## Deliverables

1. Interview recordings + transcripts (consent required)
2. Affinity map of themes
3. Persona refinement
4. Willingness-to-pay analysis
5. Go/No-go recommendation memo
