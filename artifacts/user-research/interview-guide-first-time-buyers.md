# PropIQ Interview Guide — First-Time Buyers

**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Format:** Video call or in person
**Interviewer notes:** This segment is less financially sophisticated. Use simpler language. The goal is to check whether they feel overwhelmed by the buying process and whether PropIQ reduces or increases that overwhelm.

---

## Pre-Interview Checklist

- [ ] Consent to record obtained
- [ ] Confirmed currently searching for first UAE property
- [ ] Incentive communicated (AED 75 gift card)

---

## 1. Warm-Up (3 min)

1. How long have you been searching for a property?
2. What's driving the decision to buy now rather than rent?
3. Which areas or communities are you considering?

---

## 2. Current Experience — Problem Discovery (8 min)

*Goal: Understand the emotional and practical pain of being a first-time buyer in the UAE market.*

4. Describe what a typical week of property searching looks like for you right now.

5. What tools or websites are you using? *(Probe: Bayut, Propertyfinder, WhatsApp groups, agents?)*

6. What's the most confusing or stressful part of the whole process?

7. When you find a property you like, how do you figure out whether the asking price is fair?

8. Have you ever felt like you were being misled or that you didn't have enough information to make a confident decision? Tell me about that.

9. Do you think about what the property might be worth in 5 years? How do you try to figure that out?

10. How do you decide whether a neighbourhood is right for you beyond the listing description?

---

## 3. Concept Reveal (6 min)

> "Let me show you something. It's called PropIQ. You paste in any UAE property listing, and within seconds it tells you: (1) whether the asking price is fair compared to recent real government transaction data, (2) a neighbourhood quality-of-life score based on transport, schools, hospitals, and noise levels, (3) a forecast of what the property might be worth in 5 years, and (4) the true rental yield if you ever wanted to rent it out."

11. What's your immediate reaction?

12. Would this make you feel more or less confident making a decision? Why?

13. Which part would you look at first?

14. What would make you NOT trust the numbers it gives you?

15. Would you show this to a family member or friend before buying? Would they understand it?

---

## 4. Pricing (4 min)

16. How much would you be willing to pay per month for access to this during your property search?

17. *(After their answer)* We offer a free tier with 5 analyses per month. Does that seem like enough during a search, or would you need more?

18. If you found the perfect property and PropIQ showed the price was 15% above fair value, how would that change your decision?

---

## 5. Close (2 min)

19. Is there anything about the property-buying experience that you wish existed but doesn't?

20. Would you be open to testing a prototype? *(Get contact if yes)*
