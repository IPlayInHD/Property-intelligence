## Section 3: Concept Evaluation

> **Introducing PropIQ**
>
> PropIQ is a UAE property analysis tool. You paste any property listing URL and get an instant report with:
> - **Price Fairness Index** — compares asking price to actual DLD government transaction data
> - **Quality of Life Score** — rates the neighbourhood for transport, schools, hospitals, and noise
> - **5-Year Price Forecast** — projects property value in bear, base, and bull market scenarios
> - **True Rental Yield** — net income after service charges, vacancy, and maintenance
> - **Liquidity Score** — how quickly you could resell this property
> - **Neighbourhood Trends** — 5-year price-per-sqft history for the community
