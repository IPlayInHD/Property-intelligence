# PropIQ Participant Recruitment Screener

**Purpose:** Qualify candidates for 30-min (investors/agents) or 20-min (first-time buyers) interviews.
**Channel:** LinkedIn DMs, real estate WhatsApp groups, Bayut/Propertyfinder community forums, personal network referrals.

---

## Screener Message (DM / WhatsApp)

> Hi [Name], I'm doing research on how people make UAE property decisions. I'm looking for 10 people to do a quick 20–30 min video call and I'll send you an AED 100 gift card as a thank-you. No prep needed, just your honest experience. Would you be open to it? I have 3 quick questions to check if you're a good fit.

---

## Screener Questions

**S1.** Are you based in the UAE or have you bought/sold UAE property in the last 3 years?
- Yes → Continue
- No → Thank and disqualify

**S2.** Which of the following applies to you? *(Select one)*
- A) I actively invest in UAE real estate (2+ properties, or currently searching to buy)
- B) I am a licensed real estate agent or broker in the UAE
- C) I am searching for my first property to buy in the UAE
- D) None of the above

- A → Investor track (30-min interview)
- B → Agent track (30-min interview)
- C → First-time buyer track (20-min interview)
- D → Thank and disqualify (redirect to survey instead)

**S3.** *(Investors/Agents only)* How many UAE property transactions have you been involved in over the past 2 years?
- 0 → May disqualify (use judgment; keep if actively searching)
- 1–2 → Qualify (light)
- 3+ → Strong qualify
