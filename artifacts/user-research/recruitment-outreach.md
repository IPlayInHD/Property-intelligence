## Usage Notes

- Reddit and Facebook posts are intentionally non-promotional — avoid editing in phrases like "revolutionary" or "game-changing" as that gets flagged in these communities.
- For WhatsApp groups, always get admin permission before posting.
- The follow-up message intentionally opens an easy exit — low-pressure nudges work better in small professional networks like Dubai's property circles.
- Keep a simple log (copy the tracking sheet from `recruitment-screener.md`) of who you've messaged and when, so follow-ups don't go out twice.
