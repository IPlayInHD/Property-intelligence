# PropIQ Interview Guide — Investors & Real Estate Agents

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Format:** Video call (Zoom / Google Meet) or in person
**Interviewer notes:** Keep questions open-ended. Do NOT mention PropIQ until the Concept Reveal section. Follow the participant's lead and probe naturally. Silence is okay.

---

## Pre-Interview Checklist

- [ ] Consent to record obtained
- [ ] Screener confirmed (2+ properties / active agent)
- [ ] Incentive communicated (AED 100 gift card / coffee voucher)

---

## 1. Warm-Up (3 min)

*Goal: Build rapport, understand background.*

1. Tell me a bit about yourself — how long have you been investing in UAE property / working as an agent?
2. How many transactions have you been involved in over the past 2 years?
3. Which areas in the UAE do you focus on most?

---

## 2. Current Process — Problem Discovery (10 min)

*Goal: Understand their existing workflow without leading them. Listen for pain, workarounds, and frustration.*

4. Walk me through the last time you evaluated a property before buying or recommending it. What did you actually do, step by step?

5. What tools or sources do you rely on? *(Probe: Bayut, Propertyfinder, DLD data, brokers, friends, Excel?)*

6. What's the most frustrating part of that process?

7. Have you ever paid more than you should have — or almost did — because you lacked information? What happened?

8. How do you currently estimate a fair price for a property? How confident are you in that estimate?

9. How do you assess rental yield on a property? What factors do you include? *(Probe: Do you account for service charges, vacancy, maintenance?)*

10. How do you evaluate whether a neighbourhood is good or getting better? What signals do you look at?

---

## 3. Problem Prioritisation (5 min)

*Goal: Force-rank which pain is biggest.*

11. If you had a magic wand that could give you perfect data on ONE aspect of a property decision, what would it be?
    *(Options if needed: fair price, rental yield, neighbourhood quality, future price, how quickly it'll sell)*

12. How much time do you typically spend researching a single property before making a decision?

13. Have you ever paid for a report, consultant, or data service to help with a property decision? What was it and was it worth it?

---

## 4. Concept Reveal (8 min)

*Show the PropIQ concept — ideally a live demo, prototype, or one-page visual summary of the 6 modules.*

> "I'm going to show you something we're building. It's called PropIQ — a UAE property intelligence platform. Instead of just listing prices, it analyses any property across 6 dimensions: how fair the asking price is vs. recent DLD transactions, a quality of life score for the location, a 5-year price forecast, liquidity (how fast you could resell), true net rental yield after all deductions, and neighbourhood transaction trends. You paste in a listing URL and get a full dashboard in seconds."

14. What's your first reaction to this?

15. Which of those 6 modules would be most valuable to you? Why?

16. Is there anything here you'd be suspicious of or wouldn't trust? *(Probe: Why?)*

17. Is there anything missing that you'd want?

18. Who else in your network do you think would want something like this?

---

## 5. Pricing & Willingness to Pay (5 min)

*Goal: Stress-test the pricing tiers.*

19. If PropIQ worked as described, how much would you expect to pay for it per month?
    *(Listen first. Do NOT anchor with a price yet.)*

20. *(After they answer)* We're thinking of pricing it at: Free (5 analyses/month), AED 30/month (100 analyses + forecasting + yield), AED 150/month (unlimited + liquidity score). Does that feel right, too high, or too low? Why?

21. What would need to be true for you to upgrade from Free to Analyst (AED 30)?

22. What would need to be true for you to upgrade to Pro (AED 150)?

---

## 6. Close (2 min)

23. Is there anything you expected me to ask that I didn't?

24. If we built this and it worked well, would you be interested in being a beta tester? *(Get contact info)*

25. Is there anyone else — fellow investor, agent colleague — you think I should speak with?
